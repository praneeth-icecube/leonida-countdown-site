Leonida Countdown

Privacy policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

The short version

Leonida Countdown is an unofficial fan app, made by the independent developer named on its Google Play page. It works without an account and collects nothing unless you choose to sign in. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Without an account

The countdown, checklist, cheats, regions, reminders and widget all run on your phone. Your ticks and settings are stored only on this device. Reminders are scheduled by your phone and never involve our servers.

With an account (optional)

If you create an account, we store your email address, your password (only as a secure hash, never readable), and which checklist items you've ticked. We use this only to sign you in and keep your checklist in sync across your devices. We don't sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it for anything else.

Where it's stored

Accounts and synced ticks are kept by our hosting provider, Supabase, on servers in Mumbai, India. Everything travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Like any online service, the provider's servers keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect the service, and its routine encrypted backups may hold deleted data for a short time before they're overwritten.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Account and tap Delete account. Your account and synced ticks are deleted immediately and permanently. If you no longer have the app, email icecube@icecubelabs.in from the address you signed up with and we'll delete it within 30 days. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your phone.

Children

This app is meant for adults and isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and on our website, along with the date above.
