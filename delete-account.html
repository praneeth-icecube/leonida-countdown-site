Delete your account

Last updated 19 October 2026

This page explains how to delete your Leonida Countdown account and the data stored with it. Leonida Countdown is an unofficial fan app published by [DEVELOPER NAME].

You only have an account if you chose to create one to keep your checklist in sync across phones. The app works fully without one. If you never signed up, we hold no data about you and there is nothing to delete.

Option 1: Delete it in the app

Takes effect immediatelyFastest

  1. Open Leonida Countdown and make sure you're signed in.
  2. Tap the Account icon (the circle with a person) at the top right of any tab.
  3. On the Account screen, tap Delete account. It's the red text button in the middle of the screen, below Sign out and above the About section.
  4. In the “Delete your account?” box, tap Delete account to confirm.

Your account and your synced checklist ticks are deleted immediately and permanently, and you're signed out. This can't be undone.

If the app says it can't reach the server, check your connection and try again. If it says your sign-in has expired, sign in again and then delete.

Option 2: Ask us by email (no app needed)

If you've uninstalled the app, lost your phone or can't sign in, you can ask us to delete your account.

  1. Write to icecube@icecubelabs.in from the email address you signed up with. We can't act on requests from other addresses, because we can't tell they come from the account owner.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my account.
  3. Don't include your password. You don't need to.

We'll delete the account and reply to confirm. This happens within 30 days of your email, and usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Your email addressDeleted
Your password (stored only as a secure hash)Deleted

We keep no copy of your account. We don't hold on to a copy, an archive or a “deactivated” version of it. The app has no ads or analytics, so there is no other data about you to remove. The only traces that can remain are in our hosting provider's routine encrypted backups and its security and audit logs (which may record sign-in events such as your email address and IP address). Those are kept only for as long as the provider retains them.

Ticks and settings stored on your phone are not part of your account. They stay on the phone until you uninstall the app, which removes everything stored on your phone.

Only want to remove your synced ticks?

You can keep your account and clear the ticks stored with it. Sign in, open the Checklist tab and tap Clear all ticks. This clears them on every phone signed in to your account.
